# Zoom-Out Standard (adapted from EGM `resources/document_standards/zoom-out-standard.md`)

A backlog passes if it solves the reported problems in the simplest valuable way, with clear scope and order, at the right altitude.

- **Context sufficiency:** the business outcome and why are clear; otherwise fail immediately and ask for context.
- **Root problem:** epics trace to reported pains/goals, not symptoms.
- **YAGNI:** no speculative stories; "nice to have" is not MUST.
- **Sizing and slicing:** epics are outcomes, stories are single behaviors; a thin first slice is identifiable.
- **Duplicates and overlaps** across epics and rounds are merged or explained.
- **Dependencies:** explicit, acyclic, with foundation work covered.
- **Altitude:** business behavior only; solution detail belongs to the Journey Designer.
