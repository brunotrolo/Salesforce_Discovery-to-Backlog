# Traceability Standard

A backlog passes if every item can be traced to what stakeholders actually said, and nothing important they said is missing.

## Rules
- **No source, no requirement.** An epic, story, acceptance criterion or business rule without an anchor that supports it fails. If the source only hints, it is an open question (`negocio` or `descoberta`).
- **Support levels.** `supported` (lines say it) · `overstated` (stronger/broader than the lines) · `distorted` (different or opposite) · `unsupported` (not in the lines).
- **Anchors point at the narrowest range** that holds the evidence. A whole file is not evidence.
- **Omissions.** Explicit needs, rules and constraints in the sources must appear in the backlog or in an open question.
- **No hidden decisions.** Priority, scope, product coverage and compliance obligations stated as fact must be visible in the sources.
