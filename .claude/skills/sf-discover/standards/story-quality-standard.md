# Story Quality Standard

A story passes if a Journey Designer who was not in the discovery can specify the capability without inventing business behavior.

## Rules
- **INVEST** (Independent, Negotiable, Valuable, Estimable, Small, Testable).
- **Format:** Como `persona` · Quero `want` · Para `so_that` (business outcome). The persona is a real role from the sources.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Given/When/Then, independently testable, observable, with negative paths and edge cases that the sources mention.
- **Zero-guess rule** (from EGM's zero-decision rule): no business rule, data field, error behavior or persona may be left for the next phase to invent. Missing pieces are stated rules or open questions.
- **No solution design:** no tables, fields, components or technology in stories. Subtasks only carry discovery items or decisions to confirm.
